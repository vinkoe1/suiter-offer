# Valuation snapshot

Unit prices in this app are **not live market quotes**. They are a one-time fair-market snapshot set by the family for a marriage-permission contribution calculator. Snapshot dated **September 2026**.

Do not wire these figures to APIs, tokens, or automatic updates. To change a price, edit `src/config/offer.ts`.

| Animal | App price | Rationale |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Goat | $250 | Mid-quality market animal, round number near common U.S. / mixed-market asking prices. |
| Sheep | $275 | Slightly above goat, reflecting a typical mid-quality head. |
| Cattle | $3,350 | 2026 U.S. auction median, rounded for a single adult head. |
| Horse | $7,700 | 2026 auction median for a typical sale horse (not a show / breeding outlier). |

These livestock figures are a **family-chosen snapshot** for ceremonial accounting, not a bid, appraisal, or offer to buy or sell animals.
